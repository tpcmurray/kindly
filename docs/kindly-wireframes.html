Kindly wireframes

What the game looks like from the title screen to all three endings. Layout and content are close to final. The colors change act by act: calm blue, then loud sales red, then gray, then monochrome. The Kindly logo stays calm blue in every act. Orange numbered pins match the notes under each screen. Reference: docs/kindly-tdd.md.

  • v2 adds things to do: location tabs, team assignment, levers, A/B tests, events with choices, a support inbox and outages, and The Market.
  • Home tab name by act: A Garage → An Open-Plan Office → A Glass Campus → An Empty Floor. Tabs unlock: The Team (Seed), The Market and Your Life (Series A), Growth Lab (Act 2), The Board Room (Series B).
  • v3 (re-tuned model): founder payout in the billions, late absurd purchases (a newspaper, an island, a sports team), team morale and perks, The Board Room with Act 3 cash sinks (special dividend, AI moonshot), new round sizes, and the Huddle deal paid in cash.

  • 1A big Accept and a small, quiet text link. No border, no button, muted color. Offered on the Seed round only; Series A and later term sheets have Accept alone.
  • 2One amendment only (radio buttons). The tempting money options come first and come with a clear, immediate number. The first option is pre-selected. The observer-seat option is a decoy that sounds like control: Pebble Ventures gets one board seat at Seed, and this only takes away its vote.
  • 3The dual-class option is last, in dry legalese small print, with no plain-English explanation and no visible number. Its only cost is the money option the player didn't pick: the round stays $1.5M, the valuation and stake are unchanged, and Pebble Ventures keeps its board seat. Most players who open this will pick the money, and that's the point: the hidden path starts with choosing control over an obvious reward.
